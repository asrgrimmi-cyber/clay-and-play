Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience with clay?
Not at all. The workshop is designed for complete beginners. We demonstrate every step and help you at your table throughout the session.
Can I take my creations home the same day?
Yes. You'll take your pieces home in a protective box. Clay keeps hardening for 24–48 hours, so keep them flat and away from water during that time. A care card in the box explains everything.
How many pieces will I make?
Most people finish 2–3 keychains and 2–3 fridge magnets, depending on size and detail. Quality over quantity: we'll make sure each piece is finished properly.
What should I wear? Is it suitable for kids?
Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting a little paint on. Aprons are provided. Children aged 8 and above are welcome with a booked seat; under-12s should be accompanied by an adult.
What is the early bird price?
Seats booked before the early bird deadline cost ₹599 instead of ₹699. The price shown in the booking form is always the current one.
How is my seat confirmed after paying by UPI?
After you pay and submit your UTR number, we match it with our bank record and email your confirmation, usually within a few hours. Your seat is held as soon as you submit.
What is the cancellation / rescheduling policy?
You can reschedule for free to a future batch up to 48 hours before the session. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before get a full refund; after that we can't refund, but you can transfer your seat to a friend.
