Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience with clay?
Not at all. The workshop is designed for complete beginners. We demonstrate every step and help you at your table throughout the session.
Can I take my creations home the same day?
Yes. You'll take your pieces home in a carry bag. Air-dry clay keeps hardening after the workshop: bigger sculptures need to air dry for at least 24 hours before you handle them much. Keep them flat and away from water while they dry. The care card in your bag explains everything.
How many pieces will I make?
Most people finish 2–3 keychains and 2–3 fridge magnets, depending on size and detail. Quality over quantity: we'll make sure each piece is finished properly.
What should I wear? Is it suitable for kids?
Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting a little paint on. Children aged 8 and above are welcome with a booked seat; under-12s should be accompanied by an adult.
What is the early bird price?
Seats booked before the early bird deadline cost ₹699 instead of ₹799. The price shown in the booking form is always the current one.
How will I know my payment is verified?
After you pay and tap "Yes, I've paid" and send the screenshot on WhatsApp, we check the payment by hand against our bank record. Once it's verified, you'll get a WhatsApp notification, usually within a few hours. Your seat is held until then.
What is the cancellation, refund and transfer policy?
There are no cancellations or refunds. You can transfer your seat to another person, but please tell us on WhatsApp in advance, before the workshop.
